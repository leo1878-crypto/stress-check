Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Last updated: March 18, 2026

Who we are

This service is operated by Dr. Tatiana D'Angeli, Clinical Psychologist, PhD, based in Italy. The web application at drdangeli.com ("the App") provides a nervous system self-assessment tool and personalized regulation protocols.

Contact: support@drdangeli.com

What data we collect

When you use the App, we may collect:

  • Email address — when you create an account or leave your email for updates
  • Check-in responses — your answers to the 7 daily questions
  • Body map selections — the body areas and sensations you select
  • Nervous system state — the calculated result from your check-ins
  • Exercise completion data — which exercises you completed
  • Subscription and payment data — processed securely by Stripe (we do not store your card details)
  • Referral data — your referral code and how many friends subscribed through your link
  • Device information — browser type, screen size, and similar technical data via Google Analytics

We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, or any government-issued identification.

How we use your data

Your data is used to:

  • Provide your daily check-in results, body map analysis, and personalized protocols
  • Track your progress over time (calendar, streak, milestones)
  • Send you email communications related to your check-in results and the service
  • Process your subscription payments via Stripe
  • Improve the App through aggregated, anonymized analytics

We never sell your data to third parties. We never use your data for advertising. We never share individual data with anyone outside the service.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS in the EU region) and partially cached on your device via localStorage for offline access. Payment data is handled entirely by Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

Analytics data is collected by Google Analytics (GA4) and Pinterest Tag for understanding how visitors use the App. These services may use cookies.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access your data — email us and we will provide a copy
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data — email us and we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for email communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact support@drdangeli.com.

Email communications

When you provide your email, you may receive:

  • A short series of emails related to your check-in results (3–5 emails over 1–2 weeks)
  • Important service updates (rare, only when necessary)

You can unsubscribe from all non-essential emails by replying to any email with "unsubscribe" or by contacting support. Account-related emails (password reset, subscription confirmation) cannot be unsubscribed from.

Subscriptions and payments

The App offers three subscription plans: Basic ($9.90/month), Full ($14.90/month), and Annual ($99/year). All payments are processed securely through Stripe.

Cancellation: You can cancel your subscription at any time through the Manage Subscription button in your Account tab. After cancellation, you retain full access until the end of your current billing period. No refunds are issued for partial periods.

Free trial: New users receive 3 free check-in days. No credit card is required during the trial.

Refunds: If you believe you were charged in error, contact support@drdangeli.com within 14 days of the charge. We will review each request individually.
