Privacy Policy

Angel Mind LLC · Hosted at drdangeli.com (EU / Frankfurt) · Last updated: 2026-10-13

This Privacy Policy describes how Angel Mind LLC (“we”, “us”) processes personal data when you use drdangeli.com, including the Method 4R landing pages and the check-in application, and when you make payments via Stripe.

1. Data controller

Angel Mind LLC. For privacy requests related to Method 4R checkout, use the support channel linked from drdangeli.com.

2. Data we process

  • Account and contact data: name, email, phone (when you provide them)
  • Payment metadata from Stripe (amount, currency, transaction ids — not full card numbers)
  • Technical logs: IP, user agent, approximate location derived from CDN edge
  • Product usage data inside the check-in app (progress, preferences)

3. Purposes

  • Provide the service and course access
  • Process payments and prevent fraud
  • Customer support and service messages
  • Legal compliance and accounting

4. Legal bases (GDPR where applicable)

Contract performance, legitimate interests (security, service improvement), and consent where required (e.g. marketing emails).

5. Processors / transfers

We use infrastructure and processors such as Vercel (frontend, EU edge including Frankfurt), Stripe (payments), and our application backend at bot.tatyanadangeli.com for club/bot-related webhooks where applicable. International transfers are safeguarded by appropriate contractual measures.

6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, then delete or anonymize it.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object, or port your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

8. Contact

For privacy requests, email the support address listed on the site or write via the in-app care channel.
